# The 5 Day Net

* Net lasts 5 days with 1 hour time slot each day (longer is better, 24x7 station is the best$
* Use a simple microform
* Use a group call (e.g @NET)
* Send form as MSG
* Stage form in JS8Call Status field or use JS8Spotter
* The same form is transmitted all wwek (5 days)
* At the end of the week during the two non-Net days the forms received are reviewed
* The weekly microform will contain work was heard from the previous week
* The current exchange can support 20 Net members
* The Net members have to be registered with the Net
